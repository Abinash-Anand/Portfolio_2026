Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like every web server, the host receives your IP address and the technical details of your request (the page, the time, your browser type) when you load a page, and keeps them in short-lived server logs to deliver the site and to keep it secure and running. I do not use these logs to identify visitors. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Vercel may process data outside the EU; it relies on the safeguards it publishes for that, such as standard contractual clauses.

Anonymous visit statistics

To see whether the site works, which pages are read and which projects are opened, I use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. According to Vercel’s documentation they use no cookies and do not store your IP address or any identifier that follows you from one day to the next; the results are aggregated. What is counted:

  • Page views, the page you came from (referrer) and the country, derived from the request.
  • Browser, operating system and screen class.
  • A few events: opening a project, downloading the CV, clicking a contact link, how far you scrolled (25, 50, 75 or 100 percent), which graphics quality level the 3D experience used (high, medium, low or static), and how a visit to a room of the 3D experience began.
  • An anonymous count when something goes wrong (the 3D experience could not start, or a page error occurred), without any details, so that I can notice a broken release.
  • Page-speed measurements (loading, interaction and layout stability) so that I can fix slow pages.

Events never contain your name, your IP address or anything you typed. I do not record detailed hardware information, because it can identify a device. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding and improving the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the counting scripts are not loaded at all.

Content from other sites

Fonts and all scripts are served from this site, not from a font or script provider. Project pages show the README of the project from GitHub; its images, if any, are loaded from GitHub’s servers, which then see your IP address as with any image. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and Instagram leave this site, and those services have their own privacy policies. The project data itself is copied from GitHub when the site is built, so your visit does not contact GitHub for it.

The 3D experience

The interactive experience runs entirely in your browser. The location and IP address shown in its heads-up display are fixed decoration and are not your real ones, and the request timings it shows are simulated. Sound is generated in your browser and is off until you switch it on.

If you write to me

If you email me, I receive the address and the message you send and use them only to answer you. I delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep it.
